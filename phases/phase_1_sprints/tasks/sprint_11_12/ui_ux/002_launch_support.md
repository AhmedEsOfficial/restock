## D-13.2: FAQ Content Creation (3h)

### FAQ Categories

**Getting Started**
- How do I add items to my list?
- How do I check off items?
- Can I change the quantity of an item?
- How do I delete an item?
- Can I reorder my list?

**Smart Suggestions**
- What are suggestions?
- How do suggestions know what I need?
- Why don't I see any suggestions?
- Can I turn off suggestions?
- What happens if I dismiss a suggestion?

**Meal Tags**
- What are meal tags?
- How do I tag an item?
- Can an item have multiple tags?
- What is the Meals tab?

**Privacy & Data**
- Is my data private?
- Where is my data stored?
- Can I export my data?
- How do I delete all my data?

**Troubleshooting**
- The app is slow/crashing
- My list disappeared
- Suggestions aren't working
- How do I contact support?

### Sample FAQ Entry

```markdown
## Why don't I see any suggestions?

Restock learns your shopping patterns over time. Suggestions will start appearing after you've completed about 3 shopping trips (checked off items 3 different times).

Once you've established some patterns, you'll see suggestions at the top of your list for items you might need.

If you've been using Restock for a while and still don't see suggestions, make sure you're checking off items when you buy them—this is how Restock learns!
```

## D-14.3-14.4: Monitor User Reviews (Ongoing)

### Review Monitoring Process

1. Check Play Console daily for new reviews
2. Categorize by type:
   - Bug reports
   - Feature requests
   - Praise
   - Confusion
3. Prioritize responses
4. Respond within 24 hours

### Response Templates

**Positive Review:**
```
Thank you so much for the kind words! We're thrilled that Restock is helping you with your grocery shopping. If you ever have suggestions for how we can make it even better, we'd love to hear them!
```

**Bug Report:**
```
Hi [Name],

Thank you for letting us know about this issue! We're sorry you've experienced [issue].

We've logged this and our team is looking into it. Could you email us at support@restock.app with more details about your device and what you were doing when this happened?

We really appreciate your patience and feedback!
```

**Feature Request:**
```
Hi [Name],

Thank you for the suggestion! [Feature] is definitely something we've been thinking about.

We're always looking for ways to improve Restock, and user feedback like yours helps us prioritize what to work on next.

Thanks for taking the time to share your thoughts!
```

**Confusion:**
```
Hi [Name],

Thank you for the feedback! We're sorry that [feature] wasn't clear.

Here's how it works: [brief explanation]

We're always working to make Restock more intuitive. If you have any other questions, feel free to email us at support@restock.app.
```
